Water damage in Holliston: what actually causes it

Most water damage calls in this area come from a short list of causes. Winter pipe bursts, failed washing machine and dishwasher lines, water heater leaks, roof leaks and storm water entering basements account for the large majority.

Holliston adds a few of its own. The Town of Holliston's 2024 Hazard Mitigation Plan maps flood zones along Beaver Brook and Hopping Brook, at Lake Winthrop, and along Chicken Brook and Bogastow Brook. It records significant flooding in a residential area when the Houghton's Pond dam backed up in October 2005, and it notes that Washington Street near number 1929 floods in large storms.

If you live near any of these, check your address on the FEMA Flood Map Service Center, and ask your insurer what your policy covers before the next storm.

The weather matters too. Nearby NOAA stations average about 43 to 48 inches of precipitation a year, and the Massachusetts coastal zone program places nor'easter season between October and April. Snow that melts quickly adds water against foundations, and the town's own plan flags it as a flood risk.

Signs you should call now

  • Standing water on any floor, or water coming through a ceiling
  • A burst or leaking pipe you cannot shut off
  • A sump pump that stopped during a storm
  • Sewage or a gray, cloudy liquid backing up from a drain
  • Wet drywall, bubbling paint or a ceiling that bows downward
  • A musty smell that grows after a leak that seemed to dry

If any of these describe your home, call (508) 446-9244 instead of waiting for it to dry on its own. The Massachusetts Department of Public Health advises drying wet areas within 24 hours if possible, since damp materials give mold what it needs soon after.

What to do while you wait for the crew

Stop the water

Close the fixture shutoff or the main water valve. A burst pipe will keep running until the supply is closed.

Be careful with electricity

Turn off power to wet areas only when the panel is dry and easy to reach. Otherwise call your utility or 911.

Lift and protect

Move valuables, paper and electronics to a dry room. Prop furniture legs on foil or blocks.

Photograph everything

Take photos before cleanup. They help the claim and show the crew what happened.

What affects the cost of a water damage job

We explain scope and price before work starts, because no two losses are the same. These five factors move the total the most:

  • Water category: clean, gray or contaminated
  • Affected square footage and number of rooms
  • Materials involved, from carpet to plaster to hardwood
  • Days of drying and equipment needed
  • Rebuild scope once the structure is dry

Frequently asked questions

How fast can you get to Holliston?

The line is open 24/7 and same-day response is offered when a crew is available. We do not promise a specific arrival time, because it depends on the call volume and your location.

What should I do first when water is spreading?

Shut off the source when you safely can, raise valuables off the floor and keep children and pets clear of the water. Leave the panel alone unless it is dry and easy to reach. Then call (508) 446-9244.

Does homeowners insurance cover water damage?

Sudden and accidental water damage is often covered, while gradual leaks and flood damage often are not. The state insurance division notes that flood is excluded from standard policies. Check your policy or ask your insurer.

How much does water damage restoration cost?

It depends on the water category, the square footage, the materials, the drying days and the rebuild scope. We explain scope and price before work starts. Large losses are assessed on site.

Do you handle large losses and insurance assessments?

Yes. We keep photos, readings and a written scope for each job so your adjuster has the documents. We do not promise a claim outcome.

Is water damage restoration the same as basement waterproofing?

No. We dry and repair what water damaged. Waterproofing or foundation work is a separate trade, and we say so honestly if that is what your basement needs.

Which towns do you serve?

Holliston, plus Milford, Ashland, Hopkinton, Medway and Sherborn. Each has its own page on this site.

Can I get a price over the phone?

Not an exact one, because the loss has to be seen and measured. Call (508) 446-9244, describe what happened, and scope and price are explained before work starts.
