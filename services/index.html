How the services connect

A typical loss runs through three or four of these pages in sequence. Water comes out first, then the structure is dried, then damaged materials are removed and cleaned, and finally repairs bring the room back.

Skipping a stage causes most of the repeat problems. A ceiling repaired before the cavity is dry stains again, and a basement that was dried but never cleaned after a drain backup can keep a smell for months.

Mold is the one service that sometimes stands alone. It often follows a leak that was dried too slowly, and it usually needs the moisture source fixed first.

What every job includes

Whatever the service, the starting point is the same. We ask where the water came from, how long it has been there and what it touched. Then crews use moisture meters and a thermal imaging camera to see what the eye cannot.

Scope and price are explained before work starts. We keep photos and moisture readings so your insurer has documentation, and we say so plainly when a problem is outside our work, such as a failed pipe that needs a plumber or a leaking foundation that needs a waterproofing contractor.
